## ⚠️ Eriti tähtis küsimus W2 jaoks

W2 pesad kannavad **samu pealkirju** kui W1 (Time, Listening, Attention, Presence, Trust, Connection). Kontseptuaalselt erinevad nad selle poolest, et W2 on **selle vaatevinkel, kes kannab kogu kodu** — väsinud kandja seisukoht.

Kas igast pesast on TUNDA, et see on KANDJA pilk, mitte üldine vanema pilk?

- Time → "speed vs **stillness**" (vs W1: "speed vs **slowness**")
- Listening → "advice vs **receiving**" (vs W1: "advice vs **silence**")
- Attention → "checking vs **ease**" (vs W1: "divided attention vs **undivided gaze**")
- Presence → "being nearby vs **arriving**" (vs W1: "performance vs **honesty**")
- Trust → "control vs **release**" (vs W1: "control vs **patience**")
- Connection → "fixing vs **witnessing**" (vs W1: "providing vs **being**")

Märgi siia, kui mõni paar tundub liiga sarnane → vajab ümberraamistamist.
