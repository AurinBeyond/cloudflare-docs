## Constraints
- Color palette: deep black (#000000), soft ivory (#F5F5F3), muted brass (#C5A059)
- Typography: Cormorant Garamond (headlines), Inter (body)
- No cyberpunk, horror, aggressive, dystopian, or crypto aesthetics
- No "FREE" banners, countdowns, or urgency tactics
- No external tool badges or startup signals
- Extreme whitespace / darkspace between sections
- Maximum 8 code files

## Governance — Atoms ↔ Emergent Workflow (Finalized 2026-05-17)

### Handoff Protocol
1. Atoms creates/refines frontend (copy, layout, CSS, animations, pricing, campaigns)
2. Atoms exports to IMPLEMENTATION-PACKAGE.md marked "APPROVED FOR FRONTEND IMPLEMENTATION"
3. Emergent receives package and applies ONLY to preview/staging
4. Owner reviews preview personally
5. Only after explicit "APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION DEPLOY" may preview replace live

### Atoms Allowed Scope
- Frontend visual/presentation layer only
- Copywriting, layout, spacing, typography, CSS, animations
- Visual hierarchy, pricing presentation, component styling
- Campaign pages, funnel structures, marketing assets
- Brand positioning, influencer presentation, growth frontend

### Strictly Forbidden (Locked System Core)
- Backend, FastAPI, MongoDB, auth/session systems
- ElevenLabs voice / ConvAI runtime
- Payment/webhook logic, route guards
- Room architecture, AI agent personas, voice config
- Production deployment systems, server-side business logic
- No autonomous system mutations or speculative optimization

### Brand Principles — Aurin House Principle (Permanent)
**What we ARE building:** A curated house
**Emotional core:** Peace, slowness, deep attention, sincerity, authenticity, privacy, dignity, permission to be yourself
**Luxury means:** Rarity of presence, emotional safety, silence, spaciousness, refinement, human warmth without pressure
**Experience feels like:** Entering a quiet private house, a hidden boutique hotel, a protected space outside digital noise

**Implementation principles:**
1. Atmosphere over explanation — don't overexplain features/tech, let experience communicate quality
2. No cheap urgency — no "FREE", countdowns, aggressive CTAs, growth-hack language, spam energy
3. Preserve mystery and breathing room — whitespace, pacing, typography, silence are product experience
4. Warm luxury, not cold elitism — "you may rest here" not "prove yourself worthy"
5. Grace principle — calm intelligence, grounded warmth, restraint, dignity, presence. Never oversell, rush, pressure, sound robotic/corporate
6. Privacy is a luxury signal — no tracking pressure, emotional safety as core trust signals
7. Customer-experience-first — evaluate through "How does this FEEL to a real visitor?" not "How technically impressive?"

**Applies across:** Homepage, rooms, onboarding, copywriting, typography, animations, voice tone, CTA design, marketing, future campaigns

### Cost Discipline
- Early startup phase — minimize credit waste
- Avoid unnecessary rebuilds and endless redesign loops
- Prefer lightweight, scalable, modular, cost-efficient solutions
- Every proposal must consider implementation/maintenance/runtime cost
- Elegant startup efficiency with premium presentation
