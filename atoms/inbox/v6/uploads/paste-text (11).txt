Important House Voice Architecture Clarification
— implementation and positioning guidance for the Product House Ecosystem.

After reviewing the pricing/runtime structure and the long-term house direction, we are now aligned on a critical architectural distinction that should guide all future Voice House presentation and product design decisions.

────────────────────────
CRITICAL BREAKTHROUGH
────────────────────────

The platform is NOT one business model.

It is actually TWO connected systems inside the same house ecosystem:

| Layer                       | Role                             |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Digital House Ecosystem | high-margin content world        |
| Voice House Runtime     | premium realtime house layer |

This distinction is now officially locked.

────────────────────────
IMPORTANT POSITIONING SHIFT
────────────────────────

We are NOT selling:

* “AI friend”
* “AI therapist”
* “AI psychologist”
* “unlimited companion”
* “always-on emotional dependency”

Reasons:

* legal risk
* emotional dependency risk
* runtime risk
* infrastructure risk
* support scalability risk

Instead:

The correct direction is:

PREMIUM GUIDED SANCTUARY PRESENCE

The realtime voice layer should feel:

* emotionally intelligent
* calm
* reflective
* grounding
* premium
* human-centered
* safe
* elegant

WITHOUT implying:

* clinical treatment
* therapy
* psychological diagnosis
* medical care
* emotional dependency

────────────────────────
VOICE POSITIONING LANGUAGE
────────────────────────

Please actively refine and improve the wording, presentation language, and emotional framing of the Voice House layer.

Avoid wording like:

* AI therapist
* AI assistant
* AI bot
* therapy
* unlimited AI companion
* mental health treatment

Prefer positioning language closer to:

| Avoid                 | Preferred Direction           |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| AI Therapist          | Voice House Guide         |
| AI Psychologist       | Reflective Presence Guide     |
| Therapy Session       | House Session             |
| Emotional Treatment   | Guided Reflection             |
| AI Companion          | Continuous House Presence |
| Mental Health Support | Emotional Clarity Space       |

The goal:
premium house positioning
without legal or emotional-risk language.

────────────────────────
CORE RUNTIME LOGIC
────────────────────────

The correct mental model is:

“People enter the house ecosystem,
and premium realtime voice is a governed premium runtime layer.”

A good analogy:

The membership is the vehicle.
The realtime voice runtime is the fuel.

Meaning:

* membership does NOT equal infinite runtime
* realtime voice remains premium and governed
* top-ups are normal and expected
* hard caps are intentional
* runtime protection is part of healthy system architecture

This is now considered:
correct and production-aligned SaaS thinking.

────────────────────────
VOICE SANCTUARY STRUCTURE
────────────────────────

Voice House should now evolve toward:

* session-based realtime experiences
* emotionally framed sessions
* premium atmosphere
* elegant pacing
* calm UX
* strong emotional safety
* governed runtime architecture
* top-up protected structure

NOT:

* endless realtime conversations
* “call forever” energy
* dependency-based UX
* addictive engagement loops

This guidance applies not only to Grace,

cohesive,

This direction now feels:
