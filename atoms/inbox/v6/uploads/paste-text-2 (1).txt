Important architectural clarification for the house ecosystem:

The goal is NOT to create only a closed luxury experience.

The house must contain TWO harmonized emotional layers existing within the same product world.

LAYER 1:
Curated Luxury House

* dignity
* slowness
* emotional depth
* refinement
* intentionality
* quiet luxury
* house entrance feeling

This is what the current Luxury Landing is expressing well.

BUT:

LAYER 2 must also exist:
Open Discovery House

* breathing space
* exploration
* warmth
* humanity
* curiosity
* softer discovery
* approachable emotional openness

This includes:

* Open Library
* parts of Kids Universe
* reflective/free exploration layers
* softer educational discovery
* human connection atmosphere

Important:
These two layers must NOT feel like separate products or conflicting directions.

The experience should feel like:
one house ecosystem
with deeper and softer emotional zones inside it.

The luxury layer creates:
trust, dignity, emotional grounding.

The open discovery layer creates:
breathing room, humanity, accessibility, emotional connection.

The ideal feeling is:
a refined house that still feels emotionally human and alive.

NOT:
a cold elite club.

The house should feel:
curated and valuable,
while still allowing people to breathe, explore, and feel welcome.

This balance is now one of the most important design principles moving forward.
