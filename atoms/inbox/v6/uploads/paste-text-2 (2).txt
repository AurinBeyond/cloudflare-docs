Additional architectural clarification to fully stabilize the house structure moving forward:

The separation between the Landing Layer and the House Product Ecosystem is intentional and strategically important.

This structure exists to protect the long-term stability of the house world.

IMPORTANT:

https://pure-soul-life.emergent.host
= flexible Landing / Entrance Layer

https://prulesoul.site
= stable House Product Ecosystem

The Landing Layer is intentionally allowed to evolve more dynamically:

* campaigns
* messaging refinement
* onboarding experiments
* atmosphere testing
* conversion optimization
* seasonal positioning
* first-contact experiences

Because of this, the Landing Layer may change over time.

HOWEVER:

The House Product Ecosystem is different.

The product world is meant to become:

* stable
* curated
* architecturally coherent
* emotionally immersive
* long-term consistent

This is why the systems are intentionally separated.

If something experimental changes or breaks on the Landing Layer,
it must NOT destabilize or mutate the actual house ecosystem.

This separation protects:

* the product world
* emotional continuity
* customer trust
* system stability
* house identity

Very important:
The house ecosystem is NOT meant to become a constantly-changing marketing funnel.

It should gradually mature into:
a stable digital house world.

The Landing Layer attracts and introduces.

The Product House World holds and deepens the experience.

This distinction is now officially locked.

────────────────────────
THE TWO EMOTIONAL WORLDS
────────────────────────

Inside the House Product Ecosystem, BOTH emotional layers must coexist harmoniously:

| Emotional Layer          | Purpose                                                              |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Curated Luxury House | dignity, slowness, depth, refinement, emotional grounding            |
| Open Discovery House | breathing room, exploration, warmth, imagination, emotional openness |

The goal is NOT to choose one over the other.

The house should feel:
refined and valuable,
while still emotionally human, welcoming, and alive.

This balance is one of the core long-term design principles of the entire ecosystem.

Moving forward:
all refinement and product-development decisions should respect this foundation.
