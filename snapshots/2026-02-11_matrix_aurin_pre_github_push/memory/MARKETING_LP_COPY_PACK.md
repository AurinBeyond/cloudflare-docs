## 8. What NOT to put in copy (legal-safe lock)

These words must not appear anywhere in your marketing:

- therapy, therapist, therapeutic
- treatment, treat, cure, heal, healing
- diagnosis, diagnose, disorder, disease, condition (medical)
- patient, prescription, medication
- trauma, panic attack, panic disorder, phobia, depression, anxiety disorder, PTSD
- session (in clinical sense — use "meeting", "pause", "practice")
- intervention, protocol

Replacements (already in the AI prompts and now in marketing copy):
- "self-development", "inner balance", "wellness", "self-development space"
- "intense tightness", "an old block", "a heavy season", "the body in alert"
- "rebalancing", "settling", "noticing", "putting down"
- "wanderer", "traveller", "guest"
- "meeting", "pause", "practice", "quiet room"
