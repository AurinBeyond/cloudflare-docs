# The Hearth Protocol — manuscript scaffold v0
**Target length:** 12 A5 pages
**Voice:** Anna · adult house register · quiet, late evening
**Audience:** the parent **alone**, after the children are asleep
**NOT** parent-and-child, NOT pedagogy, NOT advice

> This file is the scaffold. The page structure, the rituals to cover,
> and the tone are described here. **Anna writes the actual prose.**
> The agent prepares this skeleton so Anna can fill in the lines
> instead of starting from blank.

## Page-by-page outline

### Page 1 — Cover
- Title: **The Hearth Protocol**
- Subtitle: *A 20-minute structured wind-down for the parent who has
  been Anchor OS for the household all day*
- A single piece of visual: a small lit hearth or single lit candle
  (matching Visual Bible adult palette — deep blue background, lantern
  amber accent). NOT children's gouache.

### Page 2 — A short letter to the parent (Anna's voice)
- ~250 words. Anna's actual hand. Confessional, not instructional.
- Opens with the specific scene: the house has just gone quiet, the
  last footstep upstairs has stilled, you are sitting in the room
  where the day ends.
- Names the specific category of exhaustion this addresses (over-committed
  mind, body that has been on call for 14 hours).
- States plainly what the protocol is *not*: not therapy, not advice,
  not parenting tips, not a course.
- Closes with the promise: 20 minutes. Same protocol every night, or
  as often as needed. Owned, not rented.

### Page 3 — How to use this protocol
- 6–8 lines of practical setup.
- *"Read by lamplight or listen. The 15-min audio reads the protocol
  for you, word for word, if reading at 11pm is already too much."*
- *"Print it once. Keep it by the bed. There is no version 2."*
- *"Skip any section that does not fit tonight. The protocol does not
  break."*

### Page 4 — Minute 1 to 3: The room ends the day, not you
- The first sub-protocol.
- A small physical ritual that signals to the body that the parent role
  is offline for the next 20 minutes.
- Anna writes the exact instruction: e.g. light one specific small light,
  close one specific door, set one specific object (a stone, a cup, a
  book) in one specific place.
- The principle stated below the instruction: *"Your nervous system
  needs a discrete signal that the shift is over. The signal must be
  the same one every night."*

### Page 5 — Minute 4 to 8: The Inheritance Inventory
- The core named ritual of the protocol.
- 6 short prompts, each one a single sentence the parent answers
  silently or in writing. The worksheet (page 13–14 of the bundle PDF,
  or a separate PDF) is the writing version.
- Suggested prompts (Anna refines):
  1. *What did my parents hand me today, without saying so?*
  2. *What did I hand my children today, without meaning to?*
  3. *What did I outsource today that I would rather have done myself?*
  4. *What did I do today that I would rather have outsourced?*
  5. *What is still open between me and one specific person?*
  6. *What is one small thing the household no longer needs me to carry?*
- Anna's note below: *"None of these are graded. None of these are
  shared. They are the inventory of one specific evening. Run it again
  tomorrow if you want."*

### Page 6 — Minute 9 to 12: One loop closed on paper
- The simplest, most concrete sub-protocol.
- Pick ONE of the six inventory answers. Write it on a single line on
  any paper. Put the paper somewhere out of sight.
- The principle: *"The mind cannot close a loop it is still rehearsing.
  Writing one line on paper is the smallest version of closing it."*
- 3 short paragraphs from Anna on why this works (without leaning on
  scientific claims — the tone is "this is what I do," not "studies
  show").

### Page 7 — Minute 13 to 16: One body sweep
- A body protocol from Anna's voice — three to five sentences, slow.
- Examples of what to write: *"Find one place in the body that is still
  holding the day's first frustration. Soften that place by a small
  amount. Notice the small amount."*
- No yoga jargon. No breath counts. No "release tension." Just
  observation + small adjustment.

### Page 8 — Minute 17 to 19: The one quiet thing
- An offering, not an instruction.
- The parent chooses one quiet thing they do not normally permit
  themselves at 11pm — a book they have been carrying around, a window
  they have not opened, a cup of something specific.
- Anna's voice: *"The Anchor OS rarely chooses for itself in a day.
  This is the three minutes when you do."*

### Page 9 — Minute 20: The hearth is yours
- Closing line of the protocol.
- One sentence Anna writes that marks the end of the 20 minutes — like
  blowing out a specific candle, or saying a specific phrase, or
  closing the page.
- Below the sentence: the same single visual as the cover.

### Page 10 — When the protocol does not work
- An honest page. Anna's voice.
- *"Some nights the protocol will not close the loops. The loops will
  win. The protocol does not promise sleep. It promises 20 minutes that
  are yours, even if sleep does not arrive after them. That is enough."*

### Page 11 — The Inheritance Inventory worksheet (sneak peek)
- A reduced-size preview of the worksheet that ships as a separate PDF.
- Six prompts in serif italic, with empty space below each.

### Page 12 — Closing
- Anna's signature.
- One small line about the audio companion (page 13 of the bundle).
- The url **prulesoullife.substack.com** quietly at the bottom.

## Tone constraints (locked)

- **No "tips."** This is not a listicle.
- **No metaphors borrowed from kids' content** — no lanterns of the same
  exact wording as Polarstar, no "little star" cross-reference.
- **No "you should."** Replace every "you should" with "you might" or
  remove the line.
- **No urgency.** The protocol exists in slow time.
- **No spiritual jargon** — no chakra, energy field, alignment, or
  abundance language.
- **No tech jargon either** — no "RAM," no "killall," no "background
  processes" in the user-facing PDF. (Tech metaphors live in the
  Substack essay, NOT inside the protocol itself. Different door.)

## Visual brief for the agent (PDF designer)

- **Page size:** A5 portrait
- **Margins:** wide. ~24mm. The page should feel uncrowded.
- **Body font:** EB Garamond / Cormorant Garamond, ~11pt, ~1.45 leading
- **Heading font:** same, larger weight, 18–22pt
- **Palette:**
  - Background: warm cream (#f0e8d6 light cream, or #1c1410 if dark
    edition is offered as second variant — TBD with Anna)
  - Headings: lantern amber (#d6a560)
  - Body: deep blue or near-black (#0f1418)
- **Visual:** one small hearth/candle illustration on cover + page 9.
  Nano Banana generates with Visual Bible adult palette (NOT children's
  gouache — Anna please confirm visual direction).
- **No clip art, no stock icons, no decorative borders.**
