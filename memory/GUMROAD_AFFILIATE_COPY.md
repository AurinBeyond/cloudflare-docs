# Gumroad Affiliate Program — Setup Copy
**Created:** 2026-05-31
**Purpose:** Brand-aligned text that founder pastes into Gumroad UI
when enabling the affiliate program for each product. Anti-marketing
tone preserved — affiliates are partners, not commission-hunters.

---

## ✅ HOW TO SET UP (founder, ~10 min total)

For EACH of the 3 products (Polarstar Bedtime Stories €9, The Hearth €19,
The Alistair Bundle €39):

1. Log into Gumroad → Products → click the product
2. Open the **Share** tab (or **Affiliates** depending on the UI version)
3. Toggle **"Enable affiliates"** ON
4. Set **Commission rate: 35%** (industry standard for digital products
   priced under €100 — high enough to motivate, low enough to keep
   margin)
5. Set **Cookie duration: 30 days** (Gumroad's max)
6. **Welcome message** field → paste the corresponding text below
7. **Sign-up page description** → paste the master copy at the bottom
8. Save

**You do NOT need to invite specific affiliates by email.** The
program becomes publicly available — anyone who finds your sign-up
link can join. We will share the sign-up link via Substack + LinkedIn
once 50 organic sales are in (social proof matters here).

## 🤝 STRATEGIC LAUNCH PLAN (once founder enables this)

**Phase 1 (Today / this week):** Founder enables affiliates on all 3
products. Programme runs publicly but unannounced. Anyone who
discovers it can join.

**Phase 2 (After 50 organic sales):** Founder writes ONE Substack
essay titled "Becoming a co-keeper" — explains the affiliate
programme, links to the sign-up page. This converts existing readers
into commission-earning advocates.

**Phase 3 (After 100 organic sales):** Hand-curated outreach to 10
specific writers whose voice overlaps with the brand (use the
template at /app/memory/outreach_template.md). Mention the affiliate
programme ONLY if they ask. The relationship comes first; the
commission is the byproduct.

**Never:** post on r/affiliates, list on AffiliatePrograms.com, or
join any aggregator. These attract commission-hunters, not co-keepers.
The wrong affiliates kill the brand contrast.

## 📊 EXPECTED RESULTS (honest projections)

These are NOT guarantees. They are baseline expectations based on
digital product affiliate norms at 35% commission, €9-39 price point,
house/minimalism niche.

**Month 1 (after launch + 50 organic sales):**
- 5-12 affiliates sign up via Substack essay
- ~10-30 affiliate sales total
- ~€100-400 in affiliate-driven gross revenue
- Founder's net: ~€65-260 (after 35% commission)

**Month 6 (after consistent Substack output + Show HN):**
- 30-60 active affiliates
- ~50-120 affiliate sales/month
- ~€500-2400/month affiliate gross
- Founder's net: ~€325-1560/month

**Year 2 (mature catalogue + 200+ essays):**
- 100+ active affiliates
- 200-500 sales/month total
- ~€2k-7k/month affiliate gross
- Founder's net: ~€1300-4550/month from this single channel

The reason these numbers are conservative: house brands grow
slowly on purpose. We are not trying to win an affiliate leaderboard.
We are building a quiet, sustainable income stream that does not
require Anna to be online 14 hours a day.
