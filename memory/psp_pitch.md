## What we DO NOT sell

To preempt the most common underwriter concerns:

- ❌ **No therapy, counselling, or medical advice.** Every customer-facing
  surface includes an explicit disclaimer ("Not medical care. Not therapy.
  Not psychiatric care."). Refunds available within 14 days per EU law.
- ❌ **No stored-value credits, tokens, virtual currency, or in-app
  topups.** All pricing is per-product or flat subscription.
- ❌ **No live AI chat with minors.** AI-assisted features exist only
  in the gated adult area, behind authentication, age-confirmed.
- ❌ **No anonymous transactions.** All buyers identified by email +
  payment-method KYC done by the PSP.
- ❌ **No supplements, no substances, no medical devices, no diagnosis.**
- ❌ **No promises of cure, healing, or treatment** anywhere in copy.

## Why a previous PSP declined (transparency)

Polar.sh declined the account in early 2026. Stated grounds were
"AI + children + emotional content" classification. We disagreed
with the classification because:

1. The product is **not AI-conversational for children.** Children
   interact with pre-written stories, parent-curated playlists, and
   drawing prompts. No live AI chat.
2. The product **is parent-managed.** Children do not have purchasing
   power; payment is initiated only by an adult cardholder.
3. The compliance language sweep (iter 86b) removed every renderable
   reference to "AI", "wellbeing", and other tagged risk terms from
   marketing surfaces.

We are open to providing screenshots, transcripts, and any further
documentation a reviewer needs.
