## ✍️ The 5 rules of the "human line" (anti-AI tone)

Anna's explicit directive: *"see mitte mingil juhul ei paistaks välja AI-lik"*.

Hard rules for every text, post, email, image:

### Rule 1 — One imperfection per post
Every Aurin post must contain ONE thing AI never does:
- A typo Anna chose not to fix
- A timestamp ("at 23:14, alone in the kitchen")
- A specific small object ("the blue mug I broke in 2019")
- A sensory mismatch ("the carpet smelled like dog even though we have a cat")

### Rule 2 — No three-comma rhythm
AI loves *"calm, quiet, and warm"*. Humans say *"calm. Sort of quiet. Warm if you stayed."*
**Replace tricolons with broken rhythm.**

### Rule 3 — Never close with a call to action verb
AI closes: "Discover", "Unlock", "Begin your journey".
Aurin closes: nothing. Or one sentence that trails off.
*"The door is open. Or it isn't. Either is fine."*

### Rule 4 — Always handwritten where possible
Real Anna handwriting > Caveat font > any sans-serif > any serif. The visual
hierarchy of trust runs top→bottom. Founder photos can be slightly soft-focus
(real cameras, not phone HDR).

### Rule 5 — Name one real fear per piece of long-form
Every Anna's letter, every podcast intro, every blog post: name one specific
fear Anneli has but doesn't say out loud. (e.g. "*I'm scared my child will
remember me as the tired parent*"). AI generalises. Anna says the precise thing.
