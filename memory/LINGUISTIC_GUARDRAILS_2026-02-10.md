## 1. ❌ HARD-BANNED WORDS (will trigger the production filter)

These are **never** allowed in Aurin's voice or in marketing copy
that references Aurin's services. Using them risks the company being
classified as a regulated medical or mental-health service.

```
therapy            therapist          therapeutic       psychotherapy
psychotherapist    psychologist       psychiatrist      treatment
cure               heal / healing     diagnosis         diagnose
diagnostic         clinical           clinically        prescription
medication         patient            mental health professional
```

### 1b. Disease / Diagnosis Labels (BANNED)
```
disorder           disease            pathology         pathological
syndrome           PTSD               panic disorder    panic attack
phobia             depression         depressive        anxiety disorder
trauma             traumatic          traumatized       trauma-informed
dissociation       polyvagal          somatic experiencing
```

### 1c. Therapy-Industry Jargon (BANNED)
```
intervention       protocol           assessment        clinical evaluation
case study         compliance         dose              dosage
```

> **substitutes the entire reply** with a safe fallback. The same rule

## 5. 🌐 GLOBAL DISTRIBUTION — Channel-Specific Notes

### LinkedIn
- **NEVER** use "trauma", "mental health", "anxiety", "depression" — LinkedIn's
  algorithm flags wellness claims and reduces reach.
- **SAFE high-engagement hooks**: "the calendar that ate my sleep",
  "the question I stopped asking", "the cost of one missed dinner",
  "what a quiet 10 minutes did for a hard week".

### X / Twitter
- Same word list. Additionally: avoid "self-care" (over-saturated).
- Approved: *"a small pause"*, *"a quiet rehearsal"*, *"the room you
  step into when the room you're in won't soften"*.

### Instagram
- Carousel-first. Approved language as above.
- Avoid before/after frames (implies treatment).

### Reddit
- Comment-level only. Approved: *"this thing helped me sit with it"*,
  *"a quiet companion app I found"*. **Never** "this app cured me".

### Email / Newsletter
- All filters above apply.
- Additionally: never use disclaimers like "consult your doctor first"
  in the same paragraph as our product link. Keep them in the footer.

## 8. ✍️ Anti-AI "Human Line" rules (for outreach copy)

The Marketing Agent's outreach DMs and influencer pitches must NOT
sound AI-written. From `MARKETING_SWEETSPOTS_2026-02-10.md`:

1. **One imperfection per post** — one typo Anna chooses not to fix,
   one specific small object, one sensory mismatch.
2. **No three-comma rhythm** — replace "calm, quiet, and warm" with
   "calm. Sort of quiet. Warm if you stayed."
3. **Never close with a CTA verb** — no "Discover", "Unlock",
   "Begin your journey". Close with one sentence that trails off.
4. **Real handwriting > Caveat > sans-serif** — always prefer the
   most human visual on the page.
5. **Name one real fear per long-form piece** — the precise thing
   the reader is afraid of but doesn't say out loud.
