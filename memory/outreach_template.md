# Outreach Template — High-Signal Creator Seeding

**Purpose:** Single 3-sentence cold-reach Max can send by hand to one
high-signal thinker, writer, or founder per week. NOT a campaign.
NOT a list-blast. ONE person at a time. NO tracking pixel. NO affiliate
offer. NO ask.

**Volume:** 1 per week × 52 weeks = 52/year.
**Expected response rate:** 10–20% (5–10 real replies/year).
**Expected genuine share rate:** 5–8% (3–4 real shares/year).
**One real share by a noted writer = 50–500 site visitors.**

This is the slowest of the three distribution tracks. It is also the
one that builds permanent relationships rather than disposable
impressions.

---

## The template (verbatim — send exactly this)

> Subject: a quiet thing
>
> Hi [first name] — I built something to get away from the noise of
> the internet for tired adults at 11pm: no app, no streak, no login,
> no data harvested, just short evening stories and a slow audio room.
> It is at prulesoul.site if it is your frequency; if not, no follow-up
> from me.
>
> — Anna

---

## Why this works

- **Three sentences. No paragraphs.** Anyone who matters has 30 seconds.
  Pretending otherwise insults the recipient.
- **No ask.** No "would you share?", no "I'd love to be on your
  podcast", no "happy to send you a free copy in exchange for". The
  ask is implicit and the recipient knows it. Not stating it is the
  respect.
- **Pre-emptive escape clause.** "No follow-up from me" releases the
  recipient from the obligation to reply. Counterintuitively, this is
  what causes them to reply.
- **No tracking pixel, no UTM string.** A bare URL. If they click,
  they click. If they share, they share. You will never know who
  shared from this — that is the cost, and the cost is the point.

---

## Targeting — who actually qualifies

**YES — send to:**
- Writers with a Substack/personal blog about slow living, digital
  minimalism, parenting, or cognitive load (not "productivity gurus")
- Indie founders who have publicly written about burnout
- People who have linked or quoted Cal Newport, Tim Urban, Oliver
  Burkeman, or similar in the last 90 days
- Hacker News commenters whose comments you have personally
  bookmarked because their thinking resonates
- Authors of out-of-print books that prulesoul lineage echoes

**NO — never send to:**
- Anyone with "creator", "10x", "growth hacker" in their bio
- Wellness influencers (wrong audience, will pitch you supplements
  in return)
- Anyone you have not personally read at least 3 published pieces
  from. If you cannot quote one of their lines back to them in the
  conversation that follows, the outreach is spam.
- Anyone you found via a list, a directory, or "creators with 10k+
  followers in [niche]". This is the road to ruin.

---

## Cadence — strictly one per week

- Monday: Pick the recipient (5 min, scrolling Substack / HN / your
  own bookmarks)
- Tuesday: Read one of their pieces in full (15 min)
- Wednesday: Send the template, with no personal flourish except
  optionally adding one true sentence about why their work resonates
  (e.g. "I read your essay on grief and stayed with it for three
  days.")

If you find yourself wanting to send two in a week, do not. The
cadence is the discipline. The discipline is the brand.

---

## What to do when someone replies

- Reply in the same tone — short, no theatre, no marketing.
- Do NOT send them a link to anything. They already have the link.
- If they ask a real question, answer it.
- If they say "interesting, will check it out" — say thank you and
  do not push. You will hear back, or you will not. Either is fine.

---

## What to do when someone shares it publicly

- Like / acknowledge the share once. Privately, if possible.
- Do NOT promote the share. Do NOT screenshot the share for your own
  social channels. Do NOT quote the share in any future Substack
  essay. The moment you turn a generous share into marketing fuel,
  you are signaling that all future shares will be used the same way
  — which kills the next share.

## Log

Maintained at `/app/memory/influencer_log.md`. One row per send. Track:
- Date
- Recipient (name + URL)
- Replied? (Y/N + date)
- Shared? (Y/N + date)
- Notes (1 line)

Do NOT track open rates. Do NOT track clicks. The absence of those is
the brand promise.
