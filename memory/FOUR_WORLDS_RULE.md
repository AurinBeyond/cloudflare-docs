# THE FOUR WORLDS RULE (v1.0 — locked 2026-05-31)
**Authored by:** Anna · prulesoul
**Rationale:** A parent looking for bedtime stories does not want
"cognitive debt" essays. A Hacker News reader does not want
"Little Star." Each world gets its own door.

> "Mitu maailma ühe katuse all, aga mitte kõik segamini ühes toas."
> — Anna

This rule overrides any conflicting instruction in
`MARKETING_MASTER_PLAN.md` or `CONTENT_DISTRIBUTION_PLAYBOOK.md`.

## The Four Worlds

### 🌟 WORLD 1 — POLARSTAR KIDS
- **Voice:** warm parent → fellow parents.
- **Audience:** parents, grandparents, teachers, homeschoolers, screen-free families.
- **Platforms (allowed):** Pinterest, Instagram (parenting), Facebook parenting groups, parenting Reddits (`r/Parenting`, `r/ScienceBasedParenting`, `r/ScreenTimeFree`, `r/homeschool`, `r/AttachmentParenting`), children's book communities, mom blogs.
- **Topics (allowed):** bedtime stories, imagination, reading together, screen-free evenings, family bonding, the read-aloud ritual, illustrations.
- **Topics (forbidden):** productivity, cognitive debt, matrix, house, builders, ADHD, founders, knowledge work, focus protocols.

### 🌿 WORLD 2 — MATRIX AURIN (Adults)
- **Voice:** quiet reflective adult → other reflective adults.
- **Audience:** slow living, digital minimalism, mindfulness-without-app, late-night thinkers, journal-writers, creators.
- **Platforms (allowed):** Substack, Medium, `r/digitalminimalism`, `r/simpleliving`, `r/slowliving`, `r/mindfulness` (carefully).
- **Topics (allowed):** unfinished loops, mental clutter, attention, reflection, stillness, ritual without app, what we lose to screens.
- **Topics (forbidden):** Little Star, bedtime stories, kids' illustrations, productivity hacks, focus protocols, ADHD optimisation.

### ⚡ WORLD 3 — CLARITY RELEASE
- **Voice:** tired builder → other tired builders.
- **Audience:** knowledge workers, founders, ADHD-curious adults, deep-work practitioners.
- **Platforms (allowed):** Hacker News, Product Hunt, `r/productivity`, `r/getdisciplined`, `r/deepwork`, `r/ADHD` (carefully), Indie Hackers, LinkedIn (builder essays only).
- **Topics (allowed):** focus, overwhelm, information overload, attention management, deep work without burnout, "I deleted X and built Y."
- **Topics (forbidden):** angels, bedtime stories, parenting (unless framed as builder/parent overlap and clearly tagged), kids' content, house metaphors.

### 📖 WORLD 4 — AUTHOR ANNA / AURIN (the founder channel)
- **Voice:** the person behind everything.
- **Audience:** people who follow the maker, not the product.
- **Platforms (allowed):** Substack (Anna's main publication), Medium (cross-post), LinkedIn (long-form essays).
- **Topics (allowed):** the creator journey, lessons learned, parenting reflections, building in public, why a thing was made, what failed.
- **Topics (forbidden):** none — but the World 4 channel never directly sells. It builds trust. Product mentions appear at the end as one quiet line.

## The Four-Step Filter (before publishing anything)

Before any post, ad, comment, video, or essay leaves the building:

```
1. Identify audience.
2. Identify platform.
3. Identify which World it belongs to.
4. Create platform-native content for that World only.
```

**Never copy a campaign across worlds.**
**Never force a Polarstar mention into a Clarity audience.**
**Never put "cognitive debt" language on a Polarstar Pinterest pin.**

## THE DOOR RULE (5th principle, added 2026-05-31 evening)

> Every piece of content must answer:
> **"What door is the reader entering?"**
> If the answer is unclear, the content is not ready.

### Worked examples

🌟 **Polarstar Pinterest pin**
- Headline candidate: *"5 minutes of bedtime reading"*
- Door = **parent looking for the slow part of the evening**
- ✅ Clear → publish

⚡ **Clarity Hacker News post**
- Headline candidate: *"Why every productivity app made me more distracted"*
- Door = **knowledge worker tired of streak-based UX**
- ✅ Clear → publish

🌿 **Matrix Aurin Substack essay**
- Headline candidate: *"The cost of unfinished thoughts"*
- Door = **reflective adult lying awake at 2am**
- ✅ Clear → publish

❌ **Counter-example (rejected)**
- Headline: *"How my bedtime story platform also helps founders focus"*
- Door = ??? Parent or builder? Both? Neither knows where to step.
- ❌ Reject → rewrite as **two separate pieces**, each with its own door.

### Failure mode

A piece that tries to open two doors at once opens neither. The reader
stands in the corridor and walks away. This is how brands lose audiences
without realising it.

## Allowed cross-link patterns

The Worlds are not silos — but cross-linking only happens **inside the
website itself**, never inside outbound marketing content.

| From | To | When |
|------|----|----|
| World 1 page (Polarstar) | World 4 (Anna's Substack) | Footer: "more from the author" |
| World 2 page (Matrix Aurin) | World 4 (Anna's Substack) | Footer: same |
| World 3 page (Clarity) | World 4 (Anna's Substack) | Footer: same |
| World 4 essay | World 1/2/3 product | One quiet line at the end, only when essay topic genuinely overlaps with that product |

**Forbidden cross-link patterns:**
- Polarstar Pinterest pin → Clarity Release page (audiences do not overlap)
- HN "Show HN: Clarity" post → Polarstar PDF (would tank the post)
- Reddit `r/Parenting` comment → Matrix Aurin essay (off-topic spam)

## Frequency discipline per world

A founder publishing in all four worlds at high cadence burns out by
month 2. Real rhythm:

| World | Weekly cadence | Monthly cadence |
|-------|----------------|------------------|
| 🌟 Polarstar | 3 Pinterest pins, 1 IG post | 1 Reddit post, 1 essay in World 4 *about* Polarstar |
| 🌿 Matrix Aurin | 1 Substack essay (every other week) | 1 Reddit `r/digitalminimalism` post |
| ⚡ Clarity Release | 1 HN comment, 1 LinkedIn essay (every other week) | 1 Reddit `r/productivity` post |
| 📖 Author Anna | 1 Substack essay (one of the four worlds rotates as topic) | 1 Medium cross-post |

**Total founder time:** ~3.5 h/week (same as before, but cleanly segmented).

## Substack publication structure

**One Substack, four sections:** `prulesoullife.substack.com`
(locked 2026-05-31, Anna's handle).

```
prulesoul.substack.com
├── Section: Polarstar Letters   (World 1, for parents)
├── Section: Matrix Aurin Notes  (World 2, for reflective adults)
├── Section: Clarity Field Notes (World 3, for builders)
└── Section: From the Author     (World 4, Anna's voice)
```

Subscribers self-select which section they want. **A reader of "Polarstar
Letters" must not get a "Clarity Field Notes" email unless they opt in.**
Substack's section toggle handles this natively.

If Substack sections feel too tangled, split into 4 separate publications
later. Start with one publication + four sections — easy to split, hard
to merge.

## Voice-recording requests (when Anna needs to record audio)

Each World gets its own audio register:

| World | Vocal direction |
|-------|----------------|
| 🌟 Polarstar | warm, slow, whispered, mother reading to a child |
| 🌿 Matrix Aurin | unhurried, soft, like a quiet adult friend at 11pm |
| ⚡ Clarity Release | calm but focused, like a builder explaining a protocol |
| 📖 Author Anna | the most natural, confessional, no performance |

Never re-use a Polarstar audio file in a Matrix Aurin context. The voice
register signals to the listener which World they have entered.
