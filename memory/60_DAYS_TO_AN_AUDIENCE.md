# 60 DAYS TO AN AUDIENCE
## Day-by-day plan: Feb 26 → Apr 26, 2026

**Goal of these 60 days:** *Build a listening audience of 5,000 – 25,000
human beings who have actually heard Aurin's voice.* Paying members come
in the next 60 days, not these.

**Required prerequisite:** `/app/memory/THE_VIRAL_ENGINE.md` must be
read and Mechanic I (Viral Sentence) and Mechanic V (Emotional Hook)
chosen by Day 2.

**Constraint:** all of this works inside the 6 Aurin Brand Locks.

**Channel hierarchy:**

```
   AURIN ←→ Substack ──→ Publer (main) ──→ 11 channels
                              ↑
                           Buffer (backup)
   Meta Business Suite ──→ Meta-only (Pixel, CAPI, lookalikes)
   Ripl ──────────────→ optional short promo video at key launches
```

**Operating ratio:** 20 % build / 80 % spread, every single day,
starting Day 8.

## The single non-negotiable

Every day for 60 days, one question is answered before any work
begins:

> *"Did anyone tell someone they love about Aurin yesterday?"*

If yes — keep going.
If no for 3 days in a row — stop everything and rewrite the
Share-Trigger lines.

Because the entire point of 60 days is not to publish 8 essays.
It is to find out whether the **Living Idea** can move from one
person to the next without losing its dignity.

If it can — the next 60 days are about scaling that motion.

If it cannot — the next 60 days are about finding out why, before
spending another euro.
