## 2. What Sovereign Circle now is (and is not)

### 2.1 What Sovereign IS — purely AI architecture

A Sovereign member receives a privately provisioned AI environment
with deeper customisation, higher limits, and asynchronous
contributions from the founder when she chooses to publish them. The
"sovereignty" word is structural — the member operates inside an
isolated data perimeter, not on the shared platform.

### 2.2 What Sovereign is NOT

- ❌ Not a coaching package.
- ❌ Not live one-on-one sessions with the founder.
- ❌ Not scheduled calls.
- ❌ Not "concierge service" in the human-attention sense.
- ❌ Not therapy or consultation in any form.
- ❌ Email support is **standard platform support**, not "personal
  advisor channel". Same SLA reasoning as the public tiers, just
  prioritised in the queue.

### 2.3 Sovereign Standard — €1,890 / quarter

**What it includes:**
- Everything in House Compass.
- **Privately provisioned `Subsystem Wing`** — isolated tenant +
  sealed data perimeter + dedicated DB shard + KMS-encrypted storage.
- **One curator AI persona tuned to the member's family/firm context**
  — choose Sara, Kaelan, Grace, or Alistair. The tuning is content
  + tone + reference-vocabulary, not a new voice clone.
- **Higher fair-use ceilings** — voice dialogue does not show a minute
  counter in the UI; an internal fair-use cap protects platform
  economics (450 adult min + 300 child min per quarter, never
  client-facing).
- **Priority routing in queues** — automated, not human-time. When
  the platform is under load, Sovereign members hit ElevenLabs and
  ConvAI first.
- **Earlier access** to new rooms and features (typically 4–6 weeks
  before public release).
- **5 child profiles** (vs 3 in House Compass).
- **No interview required.** Application form + automated approval
  for first-time Sovereign applicants. Founder reviews only for
  edge-cases (e.g. business / firm accounts).

### 2.4 Sovereign Bespoke — €3,490 / quarter

**What it includes:**
- Everything in Sovereign Standard.
- **All four curators tuned** to the family/firm — full bespoke
  context layer across Sara · Kaelan · Grace · Alistair.
- **Higher fair-use ceilings** — 600 adult + 400 child minutes
  internal cap (never displayed).
- **Founder's asynchronous content channel** — when the founder
  records a podcast episode, writes an essay, or publishes a long-
  form letter, it is delivered first to Bespoke members through
  their `Subsystem Wing`, with no public release date implied. This
  is **prepared-and-published content, never live attention**.
- **Unlimited child profiles** within a single family unit (no
  commercial multi-family use).
- **Family identity inside curator dialogue** — curators may address
  the family by their chosen names and reference shared rituals the
  family has registered (a soft personalisation layer, not a memory
  override).

## 9. Three anti-bankruptcy rules (unchanged)

1. **Voice is fuel, never a right.** No bundle ships "unlimited" to
   customers. Sovereign tiers operate under internal fair-use caps
   never displayed to clients.
2. **Two firewalled wallets in every family-layer surface.** Adult
   ConvAI minutes and Aurin storyteller minutes are atomically
   separated.
3. **Dignified expiry (Roll-Forward One Cycle).** Unused recurring
   minutes carry into the next cycle only, then expire.
