# Marketing Plan Proposal — Matrix Aurin / prulesoul.site

Anna asked: *"koosta marketing plaani ettepanek, mis vöiks veel paremaks teha, et inimene saab aru milleks see on, mis on selle lopptulemus, jms."*

> Translation: marketing plan proposal — make it clearer to visitors WHAT this is, WHAT they get out of it, etc.

This proposal answers three questions:
1. **What is the visitor's problem when they land?**
2. **What does Matrix Aurin actually offer them?**
3. **How do we tell them in 5 seconds without sounding like sales?**

---

## Part 1 — The honest positioning problem

External AI reviewers and many first-time visitors don't understand what this site IS. They see:
- A beautiful house
- 5 mysterious rooms with named guides
- No clear "what do I get here"
- No identifiable human author
- "Free during launch" but for WHAT exactly?

Result: people who would love this product **bounce in 8–12 seconds** because their brain hasn't placed it in a category.

The product is unique enough that visitors NEED categorisation help. Without it, they assume:
- "Probably another AI chatbot demo" (skip)
- "Some spiritual scam" (skip)
- "An ARG / mystery puzzle" (skip)
- "A SaaS playground" (skip)

We do NOT lose visitors because the product is bad. We lose them because the **first sentence on the page doesn't tell them what bucket to put it in**.

---

## Part 2 — Anna's actual category (the truth, plain English)

This is what Matrix Aurin actually is when you strip the elegant language away:

> **A reading room with five quiet companions.**
> When life feels heavy and you need a single quiet voice to sit beside you — not advice, not a coach, not a therapist — you open the room that fits the moment. The guide listens. You leave a little lighter.

That's it. That's the entire pitch.

**What it is NOT** (and the website must stop people thinking these things):
- Not an AI chatbot demo
- Not a personality test
- Not psychic / esoteric
- Not therapy
- Not coaching
- Not a course platform
- Not "self-help"
- Not a community / social platform

**What it IS:**
- A reading-room experience designed by one author (Anna)
- Five rooms, five quiet voices, each tuned to a different kind of moment
- Available by voice OR text — wanderer's choice
- One-time presence-time top-up (no subscription required)

---

## Part 3 — The 6 marketing tweaks that move the needle (in priority order)

### 1. 🔴 Hero rewrite — one extra line

**Current homepage hero:**
> Welcome back to yourself.

**Add this directly underneath:**
> A reading room with five quiet companions.
> Sit with one when life asks too much. Take what serves you. Leave the rest.

Effect: the visitor's brain places the product in the "reading room" / "guided reflection" category within 2 seconds. Bounce rate drops.

**Risk:** ZERO. Two lines of text.

---

### 2. 🔴 "Anna's voice" 3-line paragraph

Add this between hero and first room card:

> "Built by Anna — a soul guide, hypnotherapist, and author.
> One person. No team. No investors. No AI hype.
> This room exists because I wanted somewhere quiet to send people who write to me."
>
> — Anna

Effect:
- Removes "anonymous ARG" suspicion
- Establishes single-author credibility
- The voice signals "real human"

**Risk:** ZERO. Add anywhere on homepage.

---

### 3. 🔴 Free first conversation OFFER (not free forever)

Right now the homepage says "Free during launch — every room, every voice". This is generous but creates two problems:
- Visitors don't believe it ("free forever" usually means scam OR product is dying)
- Once paid launch flips on, returning visitors feel betrayed

**Better wording:**

> Your first conversation in any room is free.
> After that, presence-time tops up from €5.

Effect:
- Clear gift
- Clear monetisation
- Sustainable framing
- Builds trust because honesty is rare in this space

**Risk:** None — Anna may need to confirm this matches her actual offer.

---

### 4. 🟡 "Who is this for / who is this NOT for" section

Right under the rooms grid, a small two-column section:

> **This room is for you if:**
> · You've tried therapy and want something quieter
> · Life is asking too much this week
> · You don't need advice — you need a listening voice
> · You prefer reading and reflecting to talking on a phone
>
> **This room is not for you if:**
> · You need emergency mental-health support → call your local crisis line
> · You're looking for medical or psychological diagnosis
> · You want a chatbot you can prank
> · You expect another self-help platform

Effect:
- Self-qualifies visitors (the ones who stay convert at 3–5x normal rate)
- Sets up the wellness disclaimer naturally
- Filters out abuse (chatbot pranksters bounce)

**Risk:** ZERO. Pure copy.

---

### 5. 🟡 One quiet testimonial (3 lines max)

If Anna has one real reader's words, place ONE quote between the rooms grid and the open-world section. Plain text, no photo, no name needed (or first name only).

Example tone:

> "I came back three nights in a row. The room didn't try to fix me.
> It just sat with me. I didn't know I needed that."
>
> — M., Estonia

Effect:
- Social proof without LinkedIn-style polish
- Matches house tone
- Real reader voice = trust signal

**Risk:** Anna needs to source one real quote. Don't fake.

---

### 6. 🟢 "After your visit" outcome line

Most visitors don't know what to EXPECT after using the product. Add a quiet 3-bullet section near the footer:

> **What people often notice afterwards:**
> · A few quiet hours of clearer thinking
> · A breath that goes deeper than this morning's
> · One small thing in their day, looked at again with softer eyes

Effect:
- Sets realistic, gentle expectation
- Promises "small" outcomes (high credibility)
- Anchors what "success" feels like for return visits

**Risk:** ZERO. Add to footer area.

## Part 4 — Channel strategy (HOW to find first 100 wanderers)

### Channels that fit (house brand)

🟢 **Quiet substack-style newsletter**
- Anna writes one letter every other week
- Format: 1 short reflection + a quiet "the rooms are open if you need"
- Slow build, deep trust

🟢 **Instagram (selective)**
- ONLY image posts with quiet copy
- NO reels, NO "swipe up", NO algorithm chase
- Voice: the same as the website
- 3 posts a week, ~15 minutes each

🟢 **YouTube long-form (very selective)**
- ONE high-quality 8-minute video: "What this is, in plain words" (Anna on camera, no editing tricks)
- One pinned video. Don't try for content velocity.

🟢 **Pinterest**
- House aesthetics travel well here
- Pin house excerpts + room invitations
- Builds passive search traffic

### Channels that DON'T fit (do not waste time)

🔴 **TikTok**
- Wrong format, wrong audience pace
- Would force compromise on brand voice

🔴 **LinkedIn**
- Wrong context (work-mode brain)
- This product needs evening / late-night intent

🔴 **Twitter / X**
- Hostile algorithm to quiet content

🔴 **Reddit promotions**
- Sub-cultures will tear this apart unless Anna spends months building organic karma first

## Part 6 — KPIs to watch (the right ones)

**DO watch:**
- % visitors who reach a room (vs. bouncing from homepage)
- % visitors who complete one conversation (≥ 90 seconds)
- % conversations that result in second-visit-within-7-days
- LemonSqueezy first-purchase conversion rate (will appear after launch)

**DON'T watch:**
- Bounce rate alone (this product expects high bounce — wrong audience self-filters)
- Time-on-page (a 30-second visitor who comes back is more valuable than 5-minute scroller)
- Social-media vanity metrics (likes / shares on Instagram)
