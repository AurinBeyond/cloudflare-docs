## The three Tony-rules of writing for Matrix Aurin

1. **Verbs over nouns.** *Sanctuary, threshold, room, presence* push the
   brain into theory. *Notice, choose, return, rest* push the body into
   motion.
2. **Contrast, not assertion.** *Welcome back to yourself* works because
   it implies you left. Pair every claim with a felt opposite.
3. **Speak to what is happening in their head, not what is happening
   in our system.** Nobody buys "architecture." People buy relief.

## The Compass voice (one line per direction)

When a page belongs to a compass direction, the line should signal it
implicitly without naming the direction.

| Direction | One-line voice |
|-----------|----------------|
| **North · Clarity** | *"See what is true, without panic."* |
| **East · Possibility** | *"Notice what could change."* |
| **South · Foundation** | *"Stand somewhere you can return to."* |
| **West · Connection** | *"You do not have to be in this alone."* |
| **Centre** | *"Welcome back to yourself."* |

## The three-layer messaging model

For every public page, the visitor should land each layer without
reading more than the corresponding amount of copy.

### Layer 1 — 10 seconds. What is this?
> A quiet room for noticing what is already shaping your life.

### Layer 2 — 30 seconds. Why would I care?
> The world offers endless advice. Most of it adds noise. This room
> offers something different: a calm place to slow down, see your own
> patterns, and choose the next step on your own terms.

### Layer 3 — 2 minutes. How does it work?
> You join a small set of structured experiences — private voice
> rooms, written letters, calm bedtime stories for the family. You
> pay only for what you use. No live AI chat with children. No medical
> claims. No stored credits. When you ask the door to close, it closes.

## Style checklist (apply to any future copy edit)

- [ ] Can a parent reading on a phone at 11pm parse this in one breath?
- [ ] Does the line use at least one verb of motion (notice, choose,
      return, walk, listen, rest)?
- [ ] Does it imply a contrast (vana → uus)? If not, weaker.
- [ ] Does it pass the 10-second test? (Stranger lands → understands.)
- [ ] Is there any PSP-flag word still present? (therapy, healing,
      wellbeing, AI as a feature, credits, tokens)
- [ ] Does it preserve Matrix Aurin brand names + "Welcome back to
      yourself"?

If any box fails → not ready for live.
