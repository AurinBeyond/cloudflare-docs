## The Sara → Grace handoff (founder's poetic insight)

> During the day, a parent moves through Sara questions:
>
> - *"Why don't I trust my teenager?"*
> - *"Why do I get irritated when my child won't listen?"*
> - *"Why do I try to carry everything alone?"*
>
> In the evening — the house is quiet, the child is asleep, the phone
> is finally down — **Grace opens**.
>
> Not "Why are we like this?"
> But "**Why am *I* like this?**"

Founder's existing parenting-audio (already recorded) is **NOT** a "Parent Audio" — it is a **Grace Evening Reflection (for parents who arrive through their parenting day).**
