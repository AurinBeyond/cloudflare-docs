## Why this was replaced

The 5-day Sprint-0 brainstorm (Anna + GPT + Norwegian AI + agent) concluded
that "Welcome back to yourself" failed the 5-second test for new visitors:

1. It tells the visitor nothing about **what this place is**.
2. It promises an emotional return without specifying the **outcome**.
3. It offers no path forward beyond a single ambient CTA.

The new hero is built on a locked 4-layer structure:
- **Layer 1** — What it is (quiet place, books + five rooms, who it's for)
- **Layer 2** — Concrete transformation (came with → leave with, 4 rows)
- **Layer 3** — Self-recognition trio (people / parents / families)
- **Layer 4** — Three CTAs with clear hierarchy (Start Here primary)

The visual background (`/luxury-hero.png`), the entrance veil animation,
and the brand whisper survive. Only the copy and CTA structure changed.
