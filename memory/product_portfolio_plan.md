# Product Portfolio Plan — Compass-Native, 3-Month Roadmap

**Founder directive (iter 86g night):** Use the 16-anchor gold + the
Compass framework to design a multi-direction product portfolio for
both kids and adults. Discipline: pick 5 (not 16), spread across 3
months, each one leverages existing content or Anna's voice.

**Locked principle:** Never launch a product whose content does not
already exist or cannot be created in <2 hours of Anna's time.

## What we are DELIBERATELY NOT building

These were in the GPT 16-anchor list. We are **NOT** doing them:

- ❌ "Impostor syndrome antidote" — too business-bro, not compass voice
- ❌ "Pre-presentation focus" — same
- ❌ "Full moon reflection" — esoteric, scares PSPs and pragmatists
- ❌ "Gratitude before lunch" — generic wellness territory
- ❌ "Social media detox" — same, also depends on user shame
- ❌ "Big fight aftermath" — too crisis-adjacent
- ❌ "Anxiety physical grounding" — health-claim risk
- ❌ Live therapist matching — never. Not what we do.
- ❌ Live AI chat for children — never. Locked in compliance.

Each of these would either drag the brand toward wellness/therapy
classification or split the founder's attention away from the four
core direction-products.

## Discipline rules for this roadmap

1. **One product launches per ~3-week cycle.** No more.
2. **Each launch requires the previous one to have ≥5 real sales** before next is built.
3. **Origin story is the highest-leverage piece — record it second** (after Little Star demo proves voice quality is sufficient).
4. **No copy refresh on existing pages during product-launch weeks.** New product = enough cognitive load.
5. **All audio products carry the same disclaimer block** that the PDF carries: *"Not therapy. Not medical care. A personal perspective."*
6. **Polarstar Kids terminology remains untouched** throughout. Adult products are sold under Matrix Aurin branding; kids products under Polarstar Kids branding. Both share the same Gumroad seller account (`Exit the Matrix. Build the Beyond.`).
7. **Anna's voice is the brand's irreplaceable asset.** Treat recording sessions like a publication date, not a chore. Quiet morning, no schedule pressure, 2 takes max per audio.
