**Author:** Anna + agent, drafted by GPT, approved by founder
**Purpose:** Single source of truth for every Polarstar illustration.
**Rule:** Every image must pass the quality test at the end. Any

## Core Visual Identity

Polarstar is not a cartoon world.
Polarstar is not a game world.
Polarstar is not a fantasy battle world.

**Polarstar is a painted storybook world.**

Every illustration should feel like a page from a beautiful children's
book that a child wants to open again before sleep.

## Master Visual Style

**Art Style:**

- hand-painted
- watercolor
- soft gouache textures
- storybook illustration
- gentle lighting
- visible brush texture
- warm atmosphere
- natural colors

**Mood:**

- peaceful
- safe
- magical but believable
- calm wonder
- quiet exploration

**Avoid:**

- Disney
- Pixar
- DreamWorks
- Anime / Manga
- Gaming graphics
- YouTube Kids style
- Neon colors
- Hyperactive compositions
- Meme expressions

## Character Design Rules

### Children

- realistic proportions
- expressive eyes
- gentle emotions
- natural clothing
- timeless appearance

**Avoid:** superhero costumes · fashion influencer style · exaggerated
faces · giant eyes · caricatures.

### Animals

- natural
- friendly
- believable

**Not:** mascot style · talking cartoon characters · comic exaggeration.

## Story-by-Story Visual Direction

### Story 01 — Little Star
- **Theme:** A small light can matter.
- **Main image:** A small golden star shining softly above a sleeping village.
- **Visual focus:** The star is small but clearly visible.
- **Atmosphere:** Moonlit sky · quiet clouds · warm light · peaceful night.
- **Emotion:** Comfort.
- **Child feels:** *"I can be small and still matter."*

### Story 02 — Moon Boat
- **Theme:** Imagination and gentle adventure.
- **Main image:** A tiny wooden boat sailing across moonlit clouds.
- **Visual elements:** Silver moon · soft clouds · calm sky · glowing reflections.
- **Atmosphere:** Dreamlike but safe.
- **Emotion:** Wonder.
- **Child feels:** *"It is safe to imagine."*

### Story 03 — Night Forest
- **Theme:** Curiosity over fear.
- **Main image:** A child standing on a forest path illuminated by lantern light.
- **Visual elements:** Warm lantern glow · tall trees · friendly woodland animals · stars visible through branches.
- **Atmosphere:** Mysterious but welcoming.
- **Emotion:** Confidence.
- **Child feels:** *"The unknown can become familiar."*

### Story 04 — Quiet Dragon
- **Theme:** Strength can be gentle.
- **Main image:** A peaceful dragon resting near a lake at sunset.
- **Visual elements:** Calm dragon · soft scales · warm sky · reflections on water.
- **Atmosphere:** Stillness.
- **Emotion:** Respect and trust.
- **Child feels:** *"Strength does not need to be loud."*

### Story 05 — Aurin Lantern
- **Theme:** Inner light.
- **Main image:** A child carrying a glowing lantern through twilight.
- **Visual elements:** Golden lantern · winding path · evening sky · distant stars.
- **Atmosphere:** Hopeful and calm.
- **Emotion:** Self-belief.
- **Child feels:** *"My light matters."*

## World Environments

Polarstar environments should repeat familiar elements:

- stars
- lanterns
- moonlight
- forests
- rivers
- meadows
- clouds
- cozy windows
- wooden bridges
- small paths

This creates recognition and belonging. A child should feel:
*"I know this world."*

---

## Illustration Quality Test

Before approving any image:

1. Does it feel like a printed storybook?
2. Would a parent happily read this before bedtime?
3. Does it feel calm?
4. Does it avoid cartoon excess?
5. Does it belong beside the other Polarstar stories?

If any answer is NO → reject and regenerate.

---

## Polarstar Visual Prompt Foundation

Use whenever generating images. **Do not deviate.**

> *"Hand-painted watercolor children's storybook illustration, soft
> gouache texture, warm gentle lighting, peaceful atmosphere,
> bedtime-friendly, realistic child proportions, timeless storybook
> art, calm wonder, beautiful printed-book quality, soft color
> palette, safe and comforting mood."*

This prompt foundation must remain consistent across all Polarstar
stories. Per-story prompts add the specific scene description on
top of this foundation, not in place of it.
