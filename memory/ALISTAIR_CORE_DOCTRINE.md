# ALISTAIR CORE DOCTRINE — Words Become Code

**Status:** Locked design law (Founder, 2026-02-08). Applies to every Alistair laboratory.
**Audience:** Internal only. Not surfaced verbatim to users — it is the *organising spine* that the lab pages express through atmosphere and copy.

---

## The transformation chain

A sentence becomes a belief.
A belief becomes a pattern.
A pattern becomes identity.
Identity becomes destiny.

Stage 1 — **The Word.** ("Be good." "You are too much." "Money doesn't grow on trees." "What will people think?" "Don't disappoint us.") Sometimes spoken only once.

Stage 2 — **Pattern formation.** The child creates meaning to stay safe. IF approval = yes THEN I am safe. IF mistake THEN shame. IF success THEN danger. IF conflict THEN hide.

Stage 3 — **The Code.** The original words disappear. The code remains.

Stage 4 — **Adult life.** People-pleasing, self-sabotage, fear of money, overworking, fear of visibility, fear of rejection, staying in unhealthy relationships, repeating patterns. The word is gone. The code runs.
