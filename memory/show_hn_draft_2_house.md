> The meditation app did not teach me to sit quietly. It taught me to sit quietly *with the app*. ## Why this works on HN

- **Opens with a confession**, not a claim. HN respects vulnerability over confidence.
- **Names the enemy by structure**, not by company name. ("apps that sold me the absence of what they promised.") No lawsuits. No tribal triggers.
- **Concrete specifics:** "React + FastAPI + MongoDB", "€0/month infra", "no analytics", "no premium tier". HN rewards specificity.
- **Stack disclosure is the ticket** — HN audience clicks through partly to read the architecture, not the brand. The brand vocabulary stays off the post and lives ONLY on the site (after the click).
- **Closes with a question to the community**, not a CTA. Comments become conversation, not testimonials.
- **Does NOT name** "Matrix Aurin", "Polarstar", "House", "Compass", "The Hearth", "Alistair". The brand vocabulary is what makes the destination compelling AFTER the click, and what would make the HN post insufferable BEFORE the click.

## First-hour ritual (critical)

The first 60 minutes after posting determine whether a Show HN reaches
the front page or dies on `/new`.

1. **Do NOT ask friends to upvote.** HN detects vote rings instantly.
   The penalty is permanent shadow-ban.
2. **Reply to the first three comments within 5 minutes each.** Engagement
   signal matters more than upvotes in the first 30 minutes.
3. **Do not link to the Gumroad pages in any reply.** Link once, in the
   post body. Replies are for conversation.
4. **If anyone asks technical questions** (stack, hosting, how the PDF is
   generated, whether you used `fpdf2`, why ElevenLabs not Coqui, etc.) —
   answer in detail. HN loves a builder who can talk shop.
5. **If anyone asks "is this AI-generated"** — the answer is:
   > "The stories are written by me. The PDF layouts are generated by a
   > Python script using fpdf2. The audio is my own voice cloned with
   > ElevenLabs (one-take recording, no AI generation of the text). Glad
   > to share the generator if useful."

## Pre-written replies for the four most likely top comments

### "How is this different from Calm/Headspace/Insight Timer?"
> Three structural differences: (1) one-time payment, lifetime access,
> no subscription. (2) No login, no account, no email harvesting beyond
> what Gumroad needs to deliver the file. (3) The interface is deliberately
> non-engaging — there is no badge, no streak, no notification, no
> "session" length tracker. It is engineered to stop competing for
> your attention the moment you close the tab. Calm and Headspace are
> excellent products in their lane. They are also engineered to retain.

### "$4.5T wellness industry — source?"
> Global Wellness Institute, 2025 report. Roughly $4.4T was global
> wellness market spend, projected to $4.5T by 2026. The number is widely
> cited and I am happy to drop the PDF link in a reply. The exact number
> is less important than the directional point — the industry is large
> and growing, and the population it serves is not getting calmer.

### "What stack did you use?"
> React (CRA frontend, port 3000), FastAPI backend (port 8001), MongoDB
> for purchase records, ElevenLabs for voice cloning, fpdf2 for PDF
> generation, Gumroad for checkout + delivery, Resend for transactional
> email, self-hosted Plausible for the counter I cannot use to remarket.
> All deployed via Kubernetes on Emergent. Total infra cost: ~€0/month
> at current volume. Happy to share the PDF generator script if useful.

### "Privacy — how do we trust it?"
> Honest answer: trust comes from showing, not claiming. (1) Open
> network tab on prulesoul.site — only first-party requests + Gumroad
> SDK on checkout pages. (2) The MongoDB stores email + purchase
> record + nothing else. (3) The site has no third-party tracking
> pixels — I can list the requests if anyone wants to verify. (4) The
> only outbound integration is Resend for purchase receipts. If anyone
> has a more rigorous audit suggestion I am open.
