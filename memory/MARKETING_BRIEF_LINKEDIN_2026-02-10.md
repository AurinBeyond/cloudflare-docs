## 🎯 The "human line" — anti-AI tone enforcement

Every post must contain at least ONE of these "fingerprints" that
AI writing tools cannot produce:

1. **A timestamp**: "at 03:14", "06:40 before the inbox", "22:50".
2. **A specific small object**: "the blue mug", "a wooden pencil",
   "the corner of the blanket", "the radiator's tick".
3. **A specific personal detail**: "at forty-one, I woke up already tired".
4. **One imperfection**: a fragment sentence, a comma where a period
   would be stricter.
5. **A trailing-off ending**: never close with a CTA verb like
   "Unlock" / "Discover" / "Begin". End with one image that dims.
