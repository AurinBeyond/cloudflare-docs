## What MUST be evaluated before any build

1. **Regulatory** — many countries restrict animal-health claims.
   Cannot say "diagnoses" or "treats" without veterinary credentials.
2. **Voice / acoustic frequency claims** — must be marketed as
   "ambient atmosphere", never "therapeutic frequency".
3. **Audience reachability** — UHNW pet community is largely word-of-
   mouth (Soho House circles, breed-specific forums). Cold marketing
   will fail; need 2-3 "patron zero" relationships first.
4. **Voice/persona design** — needs a third agent identity (not
   Grace, not Sara, not Alistair). New character work required.
