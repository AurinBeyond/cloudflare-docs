# BODY WORLD — NARRATIVE LAW
**Locked: 2026-06-16 by founder**

## THE PROTAGONIST OF BODY WORLD IS NOT THE TRAVELER.
## IT IS THE BACKPACK.

The traveler is the carrier. The backpack carries the burdens.

Each of the 14 stones is a burden carried in the backpack:
- Stone 1: what the body is trying to say
- Stone 2: what is still being carried emotionally
- Stone 3: what was inherited (body memory)
- Stone 4: what stories define identity
- Stone 5: what the body is protecting against
- Stone 6: how the body works WITH the carrier or AGAINST them
- Stone 7: the engineering of how the body operates
- Stone 8: how relationships shape the body
- Stone 9: how environment shapes the body
- Stone 10: how time is lived in the body
- Stone 11: stress + nervous system state
- Stone 12: how the body speaks before words
- Stone 13: what growth and transformation look like
- Stone 14: what kind of life is chosen — survive vs. live

The lake reflects the carrier back to themselves.

The question Body World asks:
> **"What are you carrying in your backpack?"**

Stone 14 answers:
> **"What are you ready to put down?"**

## DESIGN CONSEQUENCES

- Hub painting MUST show the backpack-carrier. Without backpack, the
  metaphor collapses.
- Stone 14 painting MUST show the carrier without burden — bubbles
  rising instead of stones (release).
- Any future hub or stone painting must preserve this arc.
- Gender, age, ethnicity of the carrier = irrelevant. We are all
  travelers. Only the backpack matters.
