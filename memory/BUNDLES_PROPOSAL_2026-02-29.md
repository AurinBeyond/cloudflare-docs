## Adult side · 4 bundle options (Matrix Aurin brand)

**Anti-wellness positioning:**
*"Not therapy. Not advice. The friend who noticed first, lent to you
by audio."*

Founder recording time: ~3 hours over one quiet weekend.

### A2 — **The 17:00 Decompression** · €9
**SOUTH · Foundation register**

Three 5-minute audio loops, Anna's voice, designed to be played
when closing the laptop at day's end. No music. No "now relax your

- "Drop today before you walk through the door"
- "Leave the screens in the screen"
- "What you carried but did not need to"

Founder recording time: ~1 hour for all three.

### A3 — **The Sovereign Operating Manual** · €29
**Composite bundle — replaces "Sovereign Mind Bundle"**

This is the agent's stronger alternative to the GPT-proposed Mind
Bundle. It is **8 days of practice**, not a folder of audio files.

**Contents (all use existing portfolio components):**
1. **3 audios from A2** (17:00 Decompression — €9 standalone value)
2. **5 audios from A1** (Quiet Friend letters — €19 standalone value)
3. **NEW · Day 8 audio: "What remains after the audit"** — single
   bonus track only inside this bundle, never sold separately
4. **NEW · 8-day Cognitive Debt Audit PDF** — one A4 page per day,
   minimal layout, Cormorant Garamond, kept printable. The audit
   itself is structured (not free-form):
   - Day 1: *"What followed me home today?"*
   - Day 2: *"What did I carry that was not mine?"*
   - Day 3: *"What did I say yes to that my body said no to?"*
   - Day 4: *"Where did I perform when I could have just been?"*
   - Day 5: *"Who noticed me today — and did I let them?"*
   - Day 6: *"What rooms did I enter by reflex?"*
   - Day 7: *"What did I refuse to want?"*
   - Day 8: *"What remains after the audit?"*

**Liturgia (ordering rule):**
- Day N → listen to Decompression #(N mod 3) + Letter #(N mod 5) +
  audit Day N
- No "shuffle" option. The order IS the practice.

**Anti-wellness positioning:**
*"This is not a meditation app. It is 8 days. No streaks. No badges.
On day 9 you close it and walk away."*

**Why €29 (not €19, not €39):**
- €19 (A1 standalone) + €9 (A2 standalone) + new audio + structured PDF
  → fair perceived value at €29
- Sub-€30 is the "I'll try it once" psychological zone for digital
  products on Gumroad
- Above €30 most buyers wait for sale or social proof

Founder time to ship: 4h (PDF layout 2h + 1 new audio 1h + Gumroad
listing copy 1h).

## What this proposal **deliberately omits** (avoid scope creep)

- ❌ No €99+ "ultimate everything" bundle — Gumroad analytics show
  these underperform vs the €29–€49 sweet spot
- ❌ No daily-streak or gamification mechanic — anti-wellness brand
  promise breaks if there's a streak counter
- ❌ No "course" framing — courses imply teaching, and Matrix Aurin
  is positioned as a quiet room, not a classroom
- ❌ No live coaching add-ons — anti-promise of the platform
- ❌ No "family + adult lifetime membership" — too ambitious for
  pre-revenue stage, brings refund complexity, and commits founder
  to recording forever
- ❌ No physical kids' product beyond F5 Journal — fulfillment
  complexity (shipping, returns, customs) is too high before Vol I
  has proven demand
- ❌ No personalised audio (e.g. "we'll say your child's name") —
  goes against the "audio is universal" brand discipline AND breaks
  Gumroad's product model

## Decision points for founder

Before any of this becomes a Gumroad SKU, founder must decide:

1. **Confirm the brand split.** Is Matrix Aurin truly the adult-only
   brand, with Polarstar Kids only for under-13s? (Audit suggests
   yes — but the X1 cross-bundle would need clear visual treatment.)
2. **Which 3 bundles ship first?** The agent recommends F1 → F2 → F3
   (family side) before any adult bundle, because audio recording
   for adult bundles takes founder out of "kids brand" headspace.
3. **Pricing tweak veto.** All prices above are agent recommendations.
   Founder can shift any of them ±€5 without breaking the structure.
4. **Liturgia structure for A3.** Is the "fixed day order" rule a
   feature or a turn-off? Some buyers want shuffle.
5. **X1 cross-bundle: launch or skip?** Strong commercial argument,
   but weakens the "two distinct brands" discipline. Founder's call.
