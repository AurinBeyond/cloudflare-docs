# AURIN FILM LANGUAGE
### The cinematic constitution of the House
### v1.0 · 2026-06-27 · Status: HELD until Phase 6

> *This is not a brief for one film. It is the language by which
> every Aurin film, keeper video, founder talk, podcast and
> promotional clip must be made — now, next year, and as long as
> the House is open.*

---

## 0 · Why this exists

Most products produce video the way they produce ads — bright, fast,
persuasive. Aurin is the opposite kind of place. The visitor came
here because the rest of the internet shouts. So every moving image
that bears the Aurin name must obey the **same rhythm, the same
voice, the same architecture** as the House itself.

This document is the cinematic constitution. It applies to:

- The House introduction film
- Keeper films (Grace · Sara · Kaelan · Alistair · Polarstar)
- Founder talks
- Podcasts (audio + video)
- LinkedIn / social videos
- Any future promotional or editorial moving image

If a film cannot pass these rules, it does not belong to Aurin —
no matter how beautifully it is shot.

---

## 1 · The six rules

### R1 — Address, never diagnose
A film may speak *to* the visitor. It may never speak *for* them.

❌ *"You stopped performing."*
✅ *"There is a room where performance is not asked of anyone."*

The visitor decides what they feel. The film offers a place.

### R2 — Invitation, not instruction
Aurin films do not tell the viewer what to do. They open a door.

❌ *"Step into your truth."*
✅ *"The door is unlocked. Come when you're ready."*

### R3 — House, not mall
The House is not a catalogue. A film must not sell rooms; it must
reveal that they exist.

❌ *"Find the room that's right for you."*
✅ *"Some rooms are still waiting for someone to open the door."*

### R4 — Equality, not chosen-ness
No visitor is more special than another. No film may use the
language of selection, secrets, or hidden truths.

❌ *"For those who can see."*
❌ *"Only a few understand."*
✅ *"Every room waits equally."*
✅ *"You choose where to begin."*

### R5 — Anna's voice, not a copywriter's voice
Every line of narration must pass the read-aloud test in Anna's
own voice. If it sounds like a copywriter reading a brand deck,
it is wrong. If it sounds like Anna speaking to one person in a
quiet room, it is right.

❌ *"Discover the platform that transforms your inner journey."*
✅ *"This is one of those places. I built it for one quiet purpose."*

### R6 — Every image must already belong to the House
A film does not invent a new world. It reveals the existing one.

❌ Stock footage of forests, oceans, sunsets, candles
❌ Generic AI-generated imagery from a different aesthetic
❌ Photos of unrelated people pretending to be visitors
✅ Painted Wider Circle worlds (already in the House)
✅ Keeper room illustrations (already in the House)
✅ Architectural details of the House itself
✅ Anna's own face, voice, and hand
✅ Founder footage filmed in real Aurin contexts

This rule is non-negotiable. The House has its own visual culture.
Importing foreign images contaminates that culture, even if the
imported images are technically beautiful.

## 2 · The aesthetic — canonical, not budget-driven

Aurin films are **illustrative, slow, hand-made-feeling, intimate**.
This is **not** a compromise made because of budget. It is the
canonical Aurin style. If one day there is a €500 000 production
budget, the films will still look this way — only with better paint,
better paper, better silence.

The painted-world look is a permanent identity choice, because:

- It signals *made by a person*, not produced by a machine.
- It rhymes with the House's own visual language (the existing Wider
  Circle worlds, room illustrations, the Anna-built feel).
- It refuses photographic realism, which would force visitors to
  evaluate "is this me?" instead of receive "this is somewhere I can go".
- It ages slowly. Photo-realistic film dates in 18 months. Painted
  work stays.

### The five canonical materials

A film may use, in combination:

1. **Slow camera moves** (pan / parallax / dolly) over still painted
   illustrations the House already owns
2. **Anna's own voice** (live or via ElevenLabs Founder Voice)
3. **One single ambient element** per film — a piano note held long, a
   distant clock, rain on a window. One, not three.
4. **Hand-set typography** — a single serif word held on screen, used
   sparingly. Never lower-third labels.
5. **Architectural light shifts** — a window brightening, a hallway
   dimming. Light is the second voice.

Anything outside these five materials needs explicit Anna approval
before entering a film.

## 3 · The rhythm

Borrowed in structure from the reference video studied 2026-06-27
(YouTube `8HQZJW50Fh4`), purified of its chosen-one framing.

### Five-beat structure

1. **A quiet question** opens the film. Not a hook. A question that
   makes the viewer recognise something.
2. **A felt recognition** — one short sentence the viewer is likely
   to silently agree with.
3. **The door appears** — a single visual that introduces the House
   without explaining it.
4. **A walk deeper** — three to five painted rooms, each held just
   long enough to breathe. Each gets one line, not three.
5. **An open end** — no call-to-action stack. One quiet sentence.
   One open door. Silence.

### Tempo discipline

- Average line length: 6–9 words
- Pauses between lines: 1.5–3 seconds (longer than feels comfortable)
- Total length:
  - House intro: 60–90 seconds (not 2–3 minutes)
  - Keeper film: 45–75 seconds each
  - Founder talk: as long as needed, but with the same pace
  - Podcast clip: native podcast length

### Sound bed

- Silence is the default sound.
- One ambient element layered at –24 dB or quieter.
- No music swells. No production-music libraries. No "epic" scoring.
- The most expensive sound in an Aurin film is silence well used.

---

## 4 · The one guiding question

When any line, any image, any cut, any sound is being chosen, the
maker asks:

> **"Does this sound like the House speaking?"**

Not:
- *Is it beautiful?*
- *Is it short?*
- *Will it convert?*

Only:
- *Does it sound like Aurin?*

Anything that does not pass this test is removed, even if everything
else about it is good.

## 5 · What this language is NOT

- ❌ Not a marketing tone-of-voice document
- ❌ Not a brand guidelines PDF
- ❌ Not a copywriting style guide
- ❌ Not a one-film brief

It is a **cinematic constitution** for the entire visual life of the
House — past, present, and future films included.

## 6 · Production order (when Phase 6 unlocks)

When Phase 6 of the launch roadmap begins, films should be made in
this order:

1. **House introduction film** — 60–90 sec. The single canonical
   piece that establishes the language for everything after.
2. **Keeper films** — one per keeper, 45–75 sec each.
3. **Founder talk** — Anna in her own room, speaking to one person.
4. **Podcast pilot** — first episode in the established voice.
5. **Social clips** — extracted vertically from the above, never
   filmed separately.

No keeper film, founder talk, or social clip may be made before
the House introduction film exists. The introduction sets the
language; the rest inherit it.
